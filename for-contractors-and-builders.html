01Who this is for

Four kinds of call I get most.

01

Mechanical contractors

You have been asked to price a kitchen exhaust or a ventilation job with no design behind it, so every quote on the table is different.

  • Hood sizes, airflows and fan duties
  • Duct layout and pressure calculations
  • Make-up air and control philosophy
  • A package the whole market can price
02

Fit-out builders

The tenancy needs mechanical documentation for the building permit and the council, and the programme will not wait.

  • Preliminary concept in days, not weeks
  • Drawings and calculations for lodgement
  • Clause compliance set out plainly
  • Answers to the surveyor and the EHO
03

Fabricators and workshops

You have a sketch, a photo or a model and need drawings your shop can build from, or a calculation to back a design.

  • Shop and fabrication drawings to AS 1100
  • Weld schedules, bolt callouts, BOMs
  • Hand calculations and FEA where it counts
  • Design modifications that keep it buildable
04

Equipment suppliers

Your customer needs the surrounding engineering before your equipment can be ordered or installed.

  • Integration of your equipment into the design
  • Supports, platforms and access
  • Ventilation and services around it
  • Documents the customer's approver accepts

03Straight answers

The questions that come first.

How fast can you turn something around?

A preliminary concept for a kitchen exhaust is usually days. A full design package depends on the site data; I give you a date with the fee and hold to it. If your deadline is not realistic, I say so before you engage me, not after.

Do you install or certify the installation?

No. I do the design and the calculations as a mechanical engineer (MIEAust). Installation, commissioning and the installer's certificates are yours or your subcontractor's, and gas work is certified by a licensed gasfitter. Where a job needs sign-off from a registered practitioner, that is coordinated with one and flagged upfront.

Will you quote against me on the same job?

No. I sell engineering, not installation, fabrication or equipment. That is the point of working together.

Can you work from a sketch or a photo?

Yes, and often that is all that exists at the start. I will tell you what else I need and what I have assumed until it arrives, and every assumption is listed in the package.

Need a design before you can quote?

Send the job and the date. Free scoping call, then a fixed fee in writing, usually within 48 hours.
